## README (paste into Agentverse)

# Ghost It 👻
Stop paying for subscriptions you don't use.

## What I do
- Add up what your subscriptions really cost, per month and per year
- Spot overlaps: 3 streaming services, 2 cloud storage plans, 2 AI tools
- Show what each one costs in hours of work, and flag price rises
- Give official cancel steps and a ready-to-send cancel email
- Track what you've ghosted and check it actually stopped charging
- Tailored tips for students, couples, families and sharehouses

## How to use me
1. List what you pay for: "Netflix $19, Stan $16, Spotify $14, gym $25 a week"
2. Optional: "We're a couple" / "I'm a student" / "sharehouse of 4"
3. Get a pack: "screen pack" or "ghost everything"
4. Track: "my ghost list"
5. Update: "Stan stopped" or "Netflix still charging"

2. Who you are (optional): "We're a couple" / "I'm a student" / "sharehouse of 4"
3. Get a pack: "screen pack" / "tech pack" / "ghost everything"
4. Track: "my ghost list"
5. Update: "Stan stopped" / "Netflix still charging"
